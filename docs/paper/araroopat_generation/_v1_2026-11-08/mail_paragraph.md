One more set of runs is planned before the paper is complete. Reviewers will ask how AraRooPat compares, beyond our own BPE baselines and each model's own vocabulary, with the tokenizers that Arabic models actually ship, and with the standard morphology-aware method. So we will put three more tokenizers through exactly the same pipeline as AraRooPat, on the same three base models, and judge them the same way: the released tokenizer of Jais-2, the tokenizer that comes with Fanar-1, and a Farasa-based BPE of the same size as our smaller BPE, which we train ourselves. The Fanar team's morphology-aware tokenizer ("MorphBPE") was never released, so ours stands in for that idea and is labelled as ours. The Farasa tokenizer tests whether our finding (at equal size, AraRooPat writes better on two of the three models) holds for a second morphology-aware design; the two released tokenizers show how shipped vocabularies behave under the same training budget. The runs take about three days of GPU time, one job at a time, and the results go straight into the tables you already have.
